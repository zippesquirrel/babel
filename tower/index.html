The Spire

SPIRE SPIRE SPIRE

An interactive installation on human joy

Today's prompt:

What do you want to be when you grow up?

Height: 0m

What is this?

The idea behind this project is very simple. So much joy in our world is left unshared. It's a tragedy, but something I hope to fix. Imagine if all the positive things that we keep in our hearts could build a spire reaching up.

What do I do?

Write something positive down in the box below, answering the prompt. If it doesn't let you answer, try using full sentences. Please know that what you write will be seen by the school, so try to truly share joy.

Made with love (and 20 hours of work) by Max K. in 10th grade
